## Main Strategy

1. Use the solder-side photo as the copper source of truth.
2. Auto-orient, then mirror and perspective-correct the solder-side image against the component-side image.
3. Build a pad map before assigning electrical meaning.
4. Trace all copper islands into temporary net IDs.
5. Promote temporary nets into named schematic nets only after a component pinout or connector role confirms them.
6. Draw the final schematic by functional block, not by physical PCB layout.

## Functional Blocks

Initial block split:

- AC input, rectifier, fusing/protection, and bulk DC rails.
- Low-voltage regulators around `U101` and `U102`.
- Horizontal drive/output/flyback/high-voltage section around `Q202`, `Q201`, and `T202`.
- Vertical deflection around `U301`, likely an NEC `uPC1031H2` vertical deflection device.
- CRT neck / video / brightness / grid drive.
- Yoke connector and deflection coils.
- User/service adjustments: focus, brightness, vertical size, vertical linearity, width, hold, and related trimmers.

## External Reference Leads

- `uPC1031H2` appears in component searches as an NEC vertical deflection device for monochrome TV / small color TV use. This supports treating `U301` as the vertical deflection IC until the board photo or datasheet pinout contradicts it.
- Some search results for `PC1031` also describe horizontal deflection circuits, so the exact marking and package must be verified from the board before the schematic relies on a pinout.

## Safety Notes

This board includes CRT high voltage and large electrolytic capacitors. The preferred verification method is photo tracing plus unpowered continuity testing. Do not power the board for reverse engineering unless there is a specific test plan and safe discharge/probe setup.
